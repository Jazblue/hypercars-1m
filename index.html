The £50 Million Handover

Land Rover Defender — Twisted Automotive

£50m+ outcome

In 2015, Charles Fawcett placed a £8 million bet on the final Land Rover Defenders. His Yorkshire-based company, Twisted Automotive, bought 240 brand-new Defenders as production ended, then rebuilt each one into a bespoke, six-figure collector car. The gamble paid off spectacularly: by 2024, the project had generated more than £50 million.
